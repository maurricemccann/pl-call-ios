## What was built
1. **Confirm when unsure.** The recogniser now reports the weakest real word's confidence per turn (words under 3 letters skipped). Below the threshold the call says "I heard: <text>. Right?" and shows a card (I HEARD, Yes, Again, the confidence %). Yes sends it; Again drops it and listens again (PUSH re-arms the mic). He can also just say yes / again (only a whole short reply counts, so a real sentence is never eaten). Saying something else replaces the held turn. No answer in 25 s: not sent, noted in the transcript. Settings: "Confirm when unsure" toggle + "Ask below N percent sure" slider (default 50%, range 20-90%).
2. **His vocabulary.** `pl-call/vocab.py` builds the list: curated seed (his terms as he says them: Psyclaupse, Curate, colourway, glyph, GlyphGuy, PL DEV, pl-os / "P L OS", the Haul, the Forge, Composer, Town, Pick, congregation, ...) plus name-like words mined from the house instruction files. His transcripts were tried and dropped: mostly tool noise and other people's names, not fit for a public app. 109 words ship as `PLCall/Resources/vocab.txt`; refresh with `python vocab.py --write C:\dev\pl-call-ios\PLCall\Resources\vocab.txt`. Also `GET /vocab` (keyed) on serve.py serves it (cached 24 h, rebuilt in the background, never blocks); the app fetches it on connect and uses bundled + fetched + his config words (cap raised 100 -> 400).
3. **Alternatives through the channel.** Recogniser runs with alternativeTranscriptions; the app builds up to 3 whole-turn alternates (least-sure stretch swapped), sends `alt` + `conf` with each message; serve.py validates them (max 3, 300 chars, conf 0-1); channel.py puts them in meta as `alt` ("a | b | c") and `heard_conf` (only when alternates exist or conf < 0.6). channel.py INSTRUCTIONS now tell the session to read them, resolve garbled words from context ("the ball" -> "the call", "bring off all teachers" -> "turn on all features"), and ask once in plain words only if still unsure and a wrong guess costs something.
4. **Noise.** Voice processing (noise suppression + echo cancel + AGC) is an engine setting enabled in `MicTap.start` for AUTO, PUSH and music mode alike; it now logs to the Ring log if it did not take. New live mic level meter on the call screen (Settings > "Mic meter", default on; grey quiet, green on speech, red when clipping; MUTED when muted). AirPods-stem push-to-talk: a second press while he is mid-sentence ends the turn immediately (the pause rule can hold a turn open for seconds in a loud room).
